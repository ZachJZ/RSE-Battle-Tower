Battle Tower Matchups

Every Battle Tower set in Pokémon Ruby & Sapphire, checked against your team. Round 8 is battles 50–56, where the ribbons are won.

How to use this

  1. Set up your team. Click Adjust Team at the top right. For each slot, type the species, then set its level, nature, ability, item, four moves, IVs and EVs. Your summary screen shows all of these. Everything saves in your browser.
  2. Pick the game and tower. Choose Ruby / Sapphire or Emerald, then Level 50 or the high-level mode. The two games use different opponent lists.
  3. Pick the round and who's on the field. Round 1 is battles 1–7, round 8 is battles 50–56. Opponents get stronger IVs each round, so the round matters. "On the field" switches every table to that Pokémon.
  4. Use the matchup card in battle. Type the Pokémon you're facing. The card lists every set it could be and shows how each of your three handles it. "Best answer" marks the one that wins the damage race most safely: who KOs whom first, counting hits needed on the worst-case rolls and who moves first.
  5. Narrow it down as you learn more. When the opponent uses a move or reveals its item, click that chip. Sets that don't match drop out and the numbers update. "Turn order" uses who moved first. Click a chip again to undo it.
  6. Track your boosts. If your Pokémon has a setup move like Calm Mind, use the − / + counter so the damage matches your current boosts.
  7. Read the tabs. Faster lists what moves before you. Can OHKO you lists what can KO you from full. You can OHKO and Survives a hit split every set into what you KO in one hit and what you don't. Safe to set up lists what's safe to boost on. Tick "Assume critical hits" to plan for the worst luck.

Damage percentages are the lowest to highest damage roll, as a share of the target's HP. Opponents are shown at the highest IVs they can have in the round you picked.

Your team

Changes apply to every tab as you type and are saved in this browser. Level 50 mode only allows Pokémon at level 50 or below. The Battle Tower doesn't allow two of the same species or two of the same held item, and bans Mew, Mewtwo, Lugia, Ho-Oh, Celebi, Kyogre, Groudon, Rayquaza, Jirachi and Deoxys.

Opponent IVs follow the game code. In Ruby / Sapphire every opponent in a round shares one IV, from 6 in round 1 up to 31 from round 7. Each set uses the highest IV it can have in the round you picked (or in any round, with All rounds). Round 8 and later always uses IV 31. Opponents with two possible abilities are given whichever one hurts you more.

Damage uses the Gen 3 formula with damage rolls from 85% to 100%. In Gen 3, Fire, Water, Grass, Electric, Ice, Psychic, Dragon and Dark moves are special; every other type is physical. Counted: Choice Band, type-boosting items, Intimidate, Levitate, Thick Fat, Huge Power, Hustle, the Absorb abilities, Wonder Guard, Sturdy against OHKO moves, multi-hit moves, Hidden Power from your IVs, and Explosion halving Defense. Not counted: Focus Band, Light Screen and Reflect, BrightPowder, weather, Leftovers recovery, and secondary effects like stat drops.

Emerald uses the Battle Frontier list: each trainer has their own pool of sets, IVs go from 3 in round 1 to 31 from round 7, and Salon Maiden Anabel appears at battle 35 (Silver) and battle 70 and every 35 after (Gold). Some legendary, Dragonite and Tyranitar sets only appear in Open Level. Singles only; doubles and multi battles aren't covered.
